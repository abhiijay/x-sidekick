# Beamcite

> The second business we work on (alongside SupaSidebar). This file is the product profile + cached facts. For where everything is stored, see [[projects/Beamcite/INDEX]].

## Entity one-liner
Beamcite is a human-supervised AI content service that gets businesses cited and recommended by AI assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini). It writes the blogs that make a SaaS show up inside AI answers. Tagline: "Be the answer AI gives."

## What they sell (the promise)
"We write the blogs that get your SaaS cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini in 30 days, or we work free until it is." Free sample in 24h. Currently in founding-spot phase (3 of 5 spots left as of Jun 2026).

## The core insight (their differentiator)
GEO = Generative Engine Optimization. Getting cited inside an AI answer depends heavily on **what other sites say about you**, often more than your own page does. An answer engine cross-checks before naming a source. On-page work (llms.txt, schema, answer-first formatting) is table stakes; off-site mentions (directories, "best of" roundups, comparison posts) are the real lever.

## How it works (3 steps)
1. **Map the questions you can win** - 9-question intake from client; they pull winnable queries from the client's own Google Search Console data (orphan queries getting impressions but no page; queries ranking position 8-20).
2. **We write, you approve** - AI drafts under editorial direction, human editor fact-checks every claim + fixes voice, built to earn a citation not just a click.
3. **We track until you're cited** - monitor ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini for the client's name, weekly updates, keep writing until citations appear (the guarantee).

- The 4-step play (from case study): (1) find questions worth answering from existing GSC data, (2) rewrite pages answer-first so AI can quote them, (3) **get cited for what OTHER sites say about you** (the real lever), (4) keep publishing on cadence so the cluster compounds.

## Glossary
- **GEO** - Generative Engine Optimization. Getting cited/recommended by AI answer engines.
- **AEO** - Answer Engine Optimization. Making your content the answer AI engines give (they treat as overlapping with GEO).
- **Citation** - when ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini names and recommends you as a source in an answer.
