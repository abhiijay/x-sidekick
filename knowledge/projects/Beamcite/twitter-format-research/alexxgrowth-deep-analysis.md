# Part A - The voice

## A1. One-sentence take

Alex writes like an operator leaking a private growth memo: the first line creates a status gap, exact numbers manufacture authority, and the rest of the post explains the machine. Confidence: 96%.

## A2. Post architecture

The default post architecture has five layers. Confidence: 97%.

1. The hook states a result, attack, discovery, or contradiction before context. Confidence: 98%.
2. The receipt supplies age, money, views, campaign size, or a named outcome. Confidence: 98%.
3. The mechanism explains one causal model with confident, concrete language. Confidence: 96%.
4. The proof block stacks comparisons, examples, and numbers. Confidence: 98%.
5. The close becomes a verdict, challenge, direct message invitation, or next experiment. Confidence: 97%.

Alex often keeps short lines inside very long posts, so the post feels fast even when it is thousands of characters long. Confidence: 99%.

The median original has 237 characters after URLs are removed, but 27.1% of originals exceed 1,000 characters. Confidence: 100%.

The mean original length is 625.17 characters because the corpus contains many full operating manuals. Confidence: 100%.
