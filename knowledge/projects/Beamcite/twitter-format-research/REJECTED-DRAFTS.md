# Rejected drafts (RD) anti-bank

Every draft Bro rejects gets logged here with his reason, same session. The
x-post-writer reads this file at Step 3 and greps drafts against it at QC.
This is the negative few-shot set that learns Bro's taste. The EXAMPLE-BANK
anti-bank holds OTHER accounts' flops; this file holds OURS.

Entry format: the draft verbatim, Bro's reason verbatim, the extracted rule.

---

## RD-1 | 2026-07-03 | TL;DR lesson post, @k77builds

```
ur tl;dr might be the most important part of ur page. most ppl write it last, in 5 mins.

when someone asks chatgpt about ur space, it quotes the cleanest summary it finds. usually ur tl;dr.

write it for the model. my daily ai-search impressions: 1,537 -> 20,179 in 44 days (gsc).
```

**Bro's reason (verbatim):** "I actually don't like this post at all. It still
seems like AI, and the last line is kind of stupid."

**Extracted rules (now in guide QC v2.3 + L12):**
1. Bolt-on receipt: the 44-day site number was not produced by TL;DR work, so
   the last line implies causation nothing backs. Number must be OF the claim.
2. Template rhythm: three tidy beats in three lines + uniform "ur" compression
   + guru imperative pivot ("write it for the model") = AI shape even with a
   clean blacklist pass.
3. Wrong form for the idea: "chatgpt quotes ur tl;dr" is observable, so it is
   an M9 show-the-thing post (screenshot of an AI answer lifting a TL;DR), not
   a lesson post. Parked until the artifact exists.

## RD-3 | 2026-07-03 | the whole Jul-4 repurpose batch (6 X + 4 LinkedIn)

**Bro's reason (verbatim):** "I don't feel like these are something I would
write. Many of these are AI slop."

The batch passed every QC checkbox. The failure was register, not rules:
drafts imitated good-twitter-content voice with lowercase costume instead of
Kshetez's actual functional typing. Representative slop lines:

- "that answer is the new homepage. go check urs." (coined metaphor + command)
- "that's the entire shelf." (coined metaphor)
- "u weren't in the room." (aphorism coinage)
- "the flat stretch is where it's easy to give up." (motivational poster)
- "the ranked link you fought for keeps losing value, while the spoken answer
  becomes the thing buyers actually act on." (AI parallelism)
- "there's an actual study on this, not just vibes." (borrowed twitter-speak)
- "adding real citations, direct quotes and statistics lifted a page's
  visibility in ai answers by up to 40%." (study-abstract register)
- "first ~3 weeks looked like nothing. i almost stopped there." (INVENTED
  lived event - fabrication, worse than style)

**Extracted rules (guide QC + L13):**
1. THE KSHETEZ TEST is the final gate, above the checklist: put the draft next
   to 3 real Kshetez emails + GOOD-EXAMPLES.md. Any sentence that could not
   appear in those gets rewritten plainer. His register has ZERO rhetorical
   devices: no coined metaphors, no parallelism, no punchlines, no audience
   callouts ("founders,"), no quotable lines. If a phrase feels screenshotable,
   it is not him.
2. One stat per post. Two stacked external stats + source parens = newsletter.
3. Studies get retold like telling a friend ("some princeton folks tested 10k
   queries"), never paraphrased in abstract register.
4. Invented lived events are BANNED, not editable. Feelings can be adjusted by
   Bro; events (a flat stretch, an "almost quit" moment) that did not
   verifiably happen are fabrication (the RD-1 bolt-on in narrative form).
5. Rating calibration: rule-compliance does not score a post above 6. Only
   register fidelity (would he type it) earns 7+.

**RD-3 outcome (same day):** 5 worst X posts rewritten under the Kshetez test;
Bro approved X-3 (princeton retell) and X-4 (2-7 sources + question) as-is,
and X-1/X-5 after the source-jargon fix (pew/bain names dropped). Rewrite
exercise produced 5 more rules, all Bro-approved and now in the guide (Step 4
post-draft pass 11-14 + source-jargon QC check) and in
guides/human-voice-writing-guide.md Part 7.

## RD-4 | 2026-07-03 | second batch, line-level flags (posts otherwise better)

Bro: "these are better, but..." Four flagged lines:

1. `so the ranking you fought for is quietly worth about half of what it was.`
   Reason: filter word "quietly". Rule: CINEMATIC ADVERBS banned (quietly,
   silently, effortlessly, suddenly). Drama words carry no information and he
   never types them.
2. X-1 line 1 (8% vs 15% packed with two conditions): "not exactly
   understandable on the first read. It should be grade three or below."
   Rule: GRADE-3 READABILITY. One idea per sentence. One number per sentence.
   Never a condition + comparison in the same sentence. If it needs a re-read,
   it's dead.
3. `paste a url, no login, no api key.` Reason: "I would never say something
   like this, again ai." Rule: FEATURE TRIADS banned (comma-separated
   marketing rhythm). Say it as a sentence: "u just paste the url".
4. X-3 line 2 (`asking it what to buy and who to hire, then acting on
   whatever name it gives back`): "really bad... even worse." Rules: parallel
   pair constructions ("what to X and who to Y") + abstract behavior
   descriptions nobody says aloud. Human version: "people ask it what to buy.
   then they buy that."

Positive anchor: Bro LIKED X-6 (the checklist) - "easy to read and looks like
a human wrote it". Promoted to GOOD-EXAMPLES as an approved-draft reference.

**Prevention shipped (same day):** `scripts/voice_lint.py` - deterministic
linter encoding RD-1..4 mechanical tells (banned/cinematic words, foils,
negation flips, feature triads, parallel pairs, sentence length, two-numbers-
per-sentence, -ly adverbs, punchline-close heuristic, FK readability grade).
Tested on this exact batch: caught all four flagged lines. HARD flag = look
again; banned word / triad / foil = kill. Bro approval overrides borderline
length flags (X-6 calibration case).

## RD-6 | 2026-07-03 | first line of an X reply draft (queue item 1885311f, @gouthamjay8 reddit-AI-hate post)

```
half tearing it up for being AI sounds about right for reddit now
```

(first sentence of draft A; second sentence "i brace for exactly that every
time i post my own stuff there" was not flagged. OP's post: "50% of commenters
gave genuinely useful critique & other 50% tore it apart since it was AI".)

**Bro's reason (verbatim):** "The first line sounds like AI in the latest reply"

**Why it's AI:**
1. Echo-agreement (playbook 4.1, the #1 tell): "sounds about right" just restates
   the OP's own stat (50% tore it apart for being AI) back at him as agreement.
   Adds nothing he didn't say.
2. Knowing-commentator register: "...for reddit now" generalizes about how reddit
   IS, in a detached voice. Not first-person, not something Kshetez types. His
   reddit takes are declaratives about HIS experience ("reddit is a goldmine",
   "I got 64+ paid users in a week from a single post"), never armchair "that's
   just how reddit is now" narration.

**Extracted rule:** do not open a reply by restating the OP's own observation
back as a knowing "yeah, that tracks / sounds about right" line, even lowercase.
The agreement-opener is echo-agreement whatever the wording. Lead with the
first-person stake directly (the un-flagged second sentence already was the
whole reply). Redrafted to: "i brace for the AI hate every time i post my own
stuff on reddit now".

## RD-11 | 2026-07-04 | both X reply drafts unused (queue item 79662aa7, @akashkv4 "trial to weekly is fastest way to grow") — WRONG-STANCE anchor, not a style tell

**Bro's reason (verbatim):** "yeah trials are super essential acc to me. ppl want
and need to see how much value they would be getting by using the app. here is
what i posted instead of suggestions"

**Why the drafts were wrong (an anchor bleeding into a stance Bro doesn't hold):**
The OP's line bundles two separate things — the TRIAL (let people see value first)
and the WEEKLY SUBSCRIPTION pricing model. The drafts collapsed both into one
target and pushed back on the whole thing, anchoring on Bro's real
subscription→lifetime reversal ([[learnings/LRN-ssb-pricing-lifetime-over-subscription]]).

whether to offer a trial. Bro is actively PRO-trial — trials demonstrate value

was fine; the STANCE was inverted on the trial half of the post.

**Extracted rule:** when an OP bundles two ideas (here: trial + subscription
pricing), don't let one anchor push back on both. The pricing anchor
(subscription dropped sales → lifetime) covers PRICING MODEL only; it says nothing
about trials, and Bro's view on trials is the opposite (essential — users need to
see the value they'd get before paying). Separate the two before drafting. Do not
generalize the lifetime>subscription anchor into a trial-skeptic position. Add
"trials = essential (value-demonstration)" to the anchor bank as a distinct,
non-conflated stance so future drafts stop merging it with the pricing reversal.

the point. When a lesson can only be told by making Bro look bad, don't tell it —
