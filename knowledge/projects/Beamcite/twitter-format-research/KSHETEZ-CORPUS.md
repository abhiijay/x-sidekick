# Kshetez corpus (KC)

**What this is:** verbatim things Bro actually typed. Nothing in here is
paraphrased, cleaned up, or Claude-written (except the clearly-marked T2
section). Typos, missing apostrophes, and lowercase stay exactly as sent.

**Why it exists (RD-5, 2026-07-03):** the system had strong NEGATIVE signal
(REJECTED-DRAFTS, voice_lint, banned-word lists) but weak POSITIVE signal.
Rules produce checklist-compliant slop; register transfers through examples.
The Kshetez test ("would he type this?") now has a concrete referent: THIS file.

**How writers use it (x-reply-writer, x-post-writer, all founder-voice content):**
1. Before drafting, read this file. During drafting, find the 2-3 corpus
   entries nearest to what you're trying to say and imitate their SHAPE:
   sentence length, how ideas connect, where thoughts just stop.
2. Final gate: every sentence of the draft must be plausible as a line in this
   file. If no corpus entry sounds like it, rewrite plainer or cut.
3. Tier weighting: T1 (he typed it) > T3 (his rewrite of a draft) > T2
   (Claude draft he approved verbatim). When tiers disagree, T1 wins.

**Update rules (any session, same-session, like the anchor bank):**
- Bro posts a tweet/reply/comment or sends a notable email -> append to T1, dated.
- Bro rewrites a Claude draft in his own words -> append the PAIR to T3
  (draft vs his version). This is the strongest voice signal we can collect.
- Bro approves a Claude draft verbatim -> that goes to GOOD-EXAMPLES.md, NOT
  here, unless promoted deliberately to T2.
- Never edit or "fix" an entry. Never delete; if something is unrepresentative
  (e.g. he was pasting someone else's words), mark it, keep it.

**What the corpus shows (observed, not invented - re-derive if entries grow):**
- Thoughts end where they end. No landing beat, no punchline, no summary line.
- Aphorisms: zero in 39 tweets. Quotable constructions: zero.
- Questions are real asks ("can anyone guide me?", "what do you think? pls be honest.")
- Self-deprecation is factual, not performed ("I seem to be doing something very wrong")
- Numbers appear plain, no framing ("21k views / 61 Upvotes / 236 comments")
- Occasional emoji (🚀 😑), occasional "guys", "pls", "idk", "gonna"
- Typos and grammar slips are left in ("wont", "its been", "atleast", "plssss")
- Excitement = short declaratives, not intensifiers ("super cool stuff.")
