## The reply architecture

Alex's common reply has three possible parts. Confidence: 97%.

1. Recognition: the person's handle or the exact object being discussed. Confidence: 99%.
2. Position: one answer, correction, reaction, or joke. Confidence: 99%.
3. Optional proof: one number or short reason when the disagreement needs support. Confidence: 97%.

Most replies stop after part two. Confidence: 99%.

Part three appears mainly when Alex is defending competence, correcting a business claim, or pitching against a challenged alternative. Confidence: 96%.
