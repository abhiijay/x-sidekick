## EX-7 - @abhiijayVinayak / M9 show-the-thing, product demo video (Bro-written, approved 2026-07-31)

**ACCOUNT: @abhiijayVinayak, not k77builds.** Bro wrote this himself after three
assistant drafts and two judge rounds were killed. His wording won. Ships with a
91-second screen recording of the Beamcite SaaS (captions added by Bro).

```
I built a tool that writes your blog posts for you

its ourank but with lifetime access


3 steps
>Generate topics
>write the blog
>publish
```

- **Slot / move:** general feed (NOT Build in Public), M9 show-the-thing. Venue
  resolved before shape per the rule added to the account profile the same day.
- **STATUS: operator-approved wording, performance UNPROVEN.** Bro's own words:
  *"I add this as a good post. But I don't know how viral it will go, so do not
  think of it as a viral post."* Cite this entry as evidence of what Bro will
  TYPE, never as evidence of what performs. Pull metrics at 24h/7d via
  x-data-manager and annotate this entry then.
- **TYPO, do not imitate:** `ourank` is a misspelling of Outrank, flagged to Bro
  before sending. If the posted version still carries it, that is an error, not
  a voice feature. Same for `its` (should be `it's`) and the stray
  whitespace-only line above `3 steps`.
- **Why Bro's version beat the drafts:**
  - **Ruthlessly shorter.** Every assistant draft ran 3-4 built lines. Bro's
    carries the same three beats with almost no connective tissue and lets the
    video do the explaining. Confirms the "get smaller on each revision" rule.
  - **`>` is the correct Avery list marker for STEPS** (A4, 92 corpus uses),
    and the steps are bare verb phrases with zero adjectives.
  - **No price anywhere in the body.** The comparison carries the positioning,
    the number stays out. This is the distinction the second judge round
    isolated: the comparison is roughly neutral in-body, the competitor's price
    figure is what makes a post read as an ad.
  - **No hook craft, no close, no CTA.** M9 skips hook craft by design and Bro
    skipped the close entirely. The judge kept insisting on an "offer" close;
    Bro dropped it and the post got better.
- **Known exposure Bro accepted knowingly:** "its outrank but with lifetime
  access" implies the only difference is billing. Outrank also auto-publishes
  into WordPress / Webflow / Shopify / Framer, does 150+ languages and AI
  images; Beamcite exports and you mark as published. Expect a "does it publish
  to my CMS" reply. Do not repeat this parity shape in a draft without flagging
  it again.
- **Verify note (all confirmed 2026-07-31):** Beamcite SaaS is $49 once,
  lifetime, one site (`lib/site.ts`, `HomePricingPurchase.tsx`). Outrank is
  $99/mo for 30 articles a month (outrank.so, fetched live). Beamcite's own
  on-site comparison table already names Outrank, so the punch-up rule is
  satisfied. The three steps match the video exactly: Topics page ->
  Write now / Schedule -> Delivered awaiting your review -> Approve -> Export
  -> Mark as published.

## EX-1 - Night / build-in-public proof (approved 2026-06-29)

```
wrote blogs for 6 weeks and nothing. was ready to kill it tbh.

then one quietly hit 1,000+ AI-search views a day and i knew smth clicked.

since then the blog went 1,537 to 20,179 daily in 44 days. no ads. chatgpt names the product now.

the trick was just not stopping during the dead weeks.
```

- **Slot:** night (proof / pull). Layout: L2 proof drop, confession hook.
- **Close reworked 2026-06-29 (L10):** old close "the flat part is where everyone quits"
  was an observation. New close "the trick was just not stopping during the dead weeks"
  is the one concrete thing that worked - a takeaway the reader can act on.
- **Length:** ~247 chars, single tweet (account below 100 followers + non-premium 280 cap).
- **Why it worked:**
  - Hook is a lived moment + confession ("nothing", "ready to kill it tbh"), not a stat or a lecture.
  - Two-stage proof arc: the "click" moment (one blog -> 1,000+/day) THEN the big number (1,537 -> 20,179 in 44 days). The small number is the emotional turn, the big one is where it went.
  - Real short-forms worked in naturally: "tbh", "smth". Reads typed-fast.
  - Number lives inside a sentence, never floating on its own line (L3 real-poster texture).
  - Plain close ("the flat part is where everyone quits"), no crafted mic-drop.
  - On-offer: the product getting cited IS the Beamcite proof. SSB used only as genuine proof.
- **Verify note:** "1,000+/day first blog" is from Bro's memory, not a source file - confirm before posting. 1,537 -> 20,179 daily in 44 days is from the how-to-get-cited blog (own product, L5).
- **2026-07-03 annotations:** (a) "quietly" in line 2 would now FAIL the
  cinematic-adverb ban (RD-4) - approved before that rule existed, don't cite
  this line as precedent, rewrite without it if reusing the arc. (b) The
  "6 weeks flat then it clicked" arc here + EX-4 is the CANONICAL version of
  the flat-stretch story (Bro-approved memory). Any other duration ("~3 weeks")
  is wrong - a journey-batch draft invented one and got killed for it (RD-3).

## EX-2 - Morning / targeted contrarian hook (approved 2026-06-29)

```
ur competitor keeps showing up in chatgpt and ur invisible. its not because their site is bigger.

i ran the play on a tiny local biz, fewer pages than anyone. couple weeks later chatgpt names them for their city, over the big guys.

answer the exact question better than the big sites do, then go get mentioned where ur buyers already hang out. thats it.
```

- **Slot:** morning (reach / teach). Layout: L3 reveal + targeted contrarian hook.
- **Close reworked 2026-06-29 (L10):** old close "its the clearest answer that wins,
  not the biggest site" was an abstract slogan. New close gives two concrete actions
  (answer the exact question better + earn off-site mentions) the reader can do. "thats it"
  is the human plain-close. NOT a "my rule:" stamp - phrased naturally for this post.
- **Length:** ~280 chars, single tweet. (Bro preferred the untrimmed version - do not over-trim a hook that's working.)
- **Why it worked (this took ~6 rewrites; the misses are the lesson):**
  - **Hook targets a specific reader and their fear (P3 identity).** "ur competitor keeps showing up in chatgpt and ur invisible" speaks straight to the invisible founder. "invisible" is the attention-pulling fear word doing the heavy lift. A neutral third-party anecdote ("ran the play on a biz...") has NO target and NO hook - that was the failed v8.
  - **Reason-to-read baked in.** "its not because their site is bigger" promises a surprising answer to their pain, so they keep reading to find what it IS (P2 curiosity gap).
  - Hook is ONE short clause of pain + ONE short contradiction. Not two stacked clauses.
  - Credential early (P4): "i ran the play on them." Shareable close (P5): "clearest answer wins, not biggest site."
  - Short forms (ur, biz), dropped apostrophes. No negation flip, no em-dash.
  - On-offer: "clearest answer" IS the on-page writing Beamcite sells. Does NOT argue against the product (the failed v3/v4 "off-site beats on-page" undercut the offer).
  - **No emoji.** This post has no strong emotional beat, so an emoji would be decoration. Emojis carry EMOTION, not attention - use only when the moment is genuinely frustrated / triumphant / surprised.
- **Verify note:** uses the local-service-biz proof from beamcite.md (different from the SSB number, so no collision with EX-1). "couple weeks" is soft - beamcite.md gives no exact timeframe for the local biz. Pin a real figure if available.

## REF-1 - A human-written tweet to study (NOT ours, reference only)

Bro flagged this as unmistakably human. Study it; do not copy the topic (it's a
journaling habit post, not Beamcite). The craft lessons are in Lesson L10.

```
been journaling every day since October 2020.

six years of daily writing thoughts, plans, gratefulness and manifestation.

i can confidently say this is one of the factors i can trace back my success to.

the level of introspection, manifestation and action that comes from written reflection is unmatched.

if ur looking for a new key habit to add to your stack, this is it.

my only rule: write one page a day, no more, no less.
```

- **Why it reads human:**
  - Hook is a flat, specific, time-anchored fact ("since October 2020"). The
    specificity IS the hook - no engineered curiosity gap. Real beats clever.
  - Earns the claim with lived duration (6 years) before the payoff.
  - Closes on ONE concrete do-able rule: "write one page a day, no more, no less."
    Copyable tonight. "no more, no less" is a human personal-constraint quirk.
  - Uneven, unforced rhythm. No list, no "3 things", no parallelism. Wanders like a person.
  - Generous, not selling. Soft rec, no product, no hard CTA.
- **The trap:** the value is the CONCRETE CLOSE, not the literal words "my only rule:".
  Do not stamp "my rule:" on Beamcite posts - that becomes our next AI tell.
