# X Stage Playbook (@k77builds)

What to post, how often, and where the effort goes at each follower stage.
Built from: EXAMPLE-BANK.md (2,600+ analyzed grower posts), the 2026
algorithm meta (growers-research.md), first-party grower writeups (Graham
Mann Feb 2026, JonBuildsHQ May 2026), and the 1,000-post journey study
(JOURNEY-*.md, deltas D1-D4 Bro-approved 2026-07-03). Vendor-sourced numbers are marked (v).

## The 2026 algorithm, in five lines

1. Replies weigh ~15x likes (v, corroborated first-party). Conversation is distribution.
2. First 30-60 min engagement velocity decides reach. Post when you can stay online.
3. Jan 2026 For You change boosts mutuals - small accounts get seen by people
   they interact with. Interaction IS the growth loop, not broadcast.
4. Grok ranks with sentiment analysis: constructive > combative. Rage-bait dying.
5. Fewer, better posts. "Daily reach budget" - low-value posts deplete it.

## Stage gates

### Stage 0: 0-500 followers (@k77builds NOW, ~5)

**The uncomfortable truth from the data: at this stage REPLIES are the strategy,
posts are the storefront.** JonBuildsHQ's peak growth day: 250 replies, 18 posts.
Graham Mann's first-party data: reply-heavy days beat post-heavy days on follows.

- Effort split: ~70% replies / 30% posts.
- Replies: 15-25/day. Target accounts 2-10x our size in GEO/SEO/BIP (see monitor
  list). Within 15 min of their post where possible. 1-3 sentences, add something
  (a number, an experience, a sharp question). NEVER generic praise.
- Posts: 1-2/day max. Allowed moves: M1 (milestone moments), M2 (anti-flex,
  business/craft numbers ONLY - never follower counts), M3 (reply-magnet questions
  WITH a finding attached), M9 (show the thing). One M4 utility list per week
  (rozzabuilds proved lists work small when COMPLETE). Mini M5 teardowns and M6
  news posts allowed when proof-carrying (value-core corollary, Bro 2026-07-02).
- **Start ONE repeatable series NOW, not at Stage 1** (journey-study delta D1,
  Bro-approved 2026-07-03): all 3 journey-studied growers ran a day-N /
  honest-number series from week 1; the series is the consistency engine, not
  a reward for having an audience.
- **THE k77builds SERIES (Bro picked, 2026-07-03): "Day N of growing beamcite
  to $1k MRR"** - L8 goal-streak format (goal-chained day-N did 2.1x baseline;
  goal-less day-N did 0.64x, banned). Every entry reports that day's REAL work
  + a real number, $0 days included. Content comes from Bro's actual day,
  never invented (RD-3 hard rule).
- **STAGE-0 FEED MIX (Bro picked, 2026-07-03, "journey spine + value
  seasoning"):** the streak post most days; ONE value post per week (M4
  complete checklist or M9 tool demo); M1/M2 moments same-day when real;
  MAXIMUM one news/stat teaching post per week. Rationale from the journey
  study: teaching-heavy feeds read as a content marketer with no story at
  small stage, journey-only also failed (Jon's void weeks) - the feed's job
  is to convert profile visits earned by replies, and for k77builds also to
  show Beamcite competence. Replies remain the actual growth engine.
- Banned: threads, "bookmark this" phrasing, generic how-to playbooks, newsletter
  CTAs, generic aphorisms, bare questions, account-meta posts (see anti-bank + L11).
- **VALUE-CORE RULE (Bro's call, 2026-07-02):** every post carries one real
  finding/number/lesson from verified work. The engagement elements (number /
  artifact / question / news / moment) are the wrapper, at least one required,
  but wrapper-without-core = don't post it. Full spec: guide Step 4 rule 4.
- **PROFILE CONVERSION SURFACE (Bro's call, 2026-07-03):** at Stage 0 the
  funnel is: reply earns the profile visit -> bio + pinned post + last 5-10
  posts convert it to a follow. Consequences: (1) the most recent 5-10 posts
  must ALL be good posts (value core, no banned genres) - one follower-count
  or diary post in the recent 10 poisons the surface; (2) pinned post = the
  account's best receipt post, kept current with positioning; (3) bio carries
  the honest numbers. Before starting a reply push, clean the recent-10 and
  set the pin FIRST - replies without a converting profile are wasted visits.

### Stage 1: 500-2,500 followers

- Effort split: ~60% replies / 40% posts. Posts 2/day (morning reach, night proof).
- Add moves: M4 utility lists (weekly), M6 news+action (as news breaks - speed wins),
  first M5 teardowns and M7 proof-of-work experiments.
- The Stage-0 series continues (started at Stage 0 per delta D1); add a SECOND
  skeleton here if the first is compounding (the mdnlabs lesson: same skeleton
  weekly builds expectation). For k77builds: "what chatgpt says about [product]"
  teardown series.
- Reply-gated giveaways allowed (R3 pattern: "reply if you want it").
- **Meta-growth content unlocks HERE, not before** (delta D4): "how I grew" /
  milestone posts need receipts (~1K followers or a real dated milestone) to
  work. JonBuildsHQ's growth articles landed at 1K-2K; identical content at
  39 followers is anti-bank material. Corollary: milestones are marketing
  assets - each one re-arms a meta post.

### Stage 2: 2,500-10,000 followers

- Effort split: 50/50, and shift reply effort into OUR comment sections (answer
  everyone, fast - extends thread visibility).
- Add: M7 authority drops as the core authority engine, short 3-6 tweet threads
  with proof screenshots (threads unlock here, NOT before), X Articles experiments.
- The Tibo/XE-1 structures (authority number -> breakdown -> CTA) start working.

### Stage 3: 10K+ - re-plan when we get there.

## The k77builds move map (what Bro actually posts)

| Move | Beamcite version | Cadence |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | First client, first AI citation for a client, revenue firsts - screenshot, one line, same day | when real |
| M2 | Honest small numbers on the road to the goal ("beamcite mrr: $X. plan says $Y by dec") | weekly-ish |
| M3 | "founders - do u even check what chatgpt says about ur product?" / one-line answerable questions | 2-3/week |
| M4 | Complete lists: "12 places that get u mentioned where AI actually looks" | 1/week |

| M8 | Contrarian GEO essay in Kshetez lowercase voice, flowing paras, lived proof | when he means it |
| M9 | Show the AI Visibility Checker / dashboards + provocative question | when shipping |
| Retro arc | Day-0-vs-day-N contrast (B32/B33 skeleton): before-list with the ugly numbers, after-list, one flat lesson line. SSB's anchor bank = 2+ years of dated receipts, available NOW | monthly-ish, re-arms with each milestone (delta D3) |

**Re-run rule (delta D2):** a proven bookmark asset (M4 list that pulled 100+
bookmarks) gets re-posted ~monthly, verbatim or lightly refreshed. rozzabuilds
data: 4 runs of the same list -> 138 / 47 / 1,219 / 137 bookmarks. Expect ~1 in
4 re-runs to catch a wave; the cost is zero new writing. Track re-runs in
x-data-manager like any post.

Voice on ALL of it: kshetez-email-voice (lowercase, blunt, short forms) - the
MoJazeem B19 register is the closest external match. Existing guide rules
L1-L10 still apply (hooks, no negation-flip, no em-dashes, verify numbers).

## Reply playbook (strategy layer; craft moved 2026-07-02)

**Craft rules now live in `REPLY-PLAYBOOK.md`** (R-moves R1-R8, anti-bank, voice,
QC - built from 1,008 real replies). This section keeps only the strategy layer:

1. Target selection: accounts 2-10x our size, GEO/SEO/BIP, ideally <15 min old
   posts. Bro picks 3-5+/day from the monitor list feed / via the sidekick extension.
2. Volume: 15-25/day at Stage 0 (~70% of total effort). Quality gates volume:
   the <1K-follower spam model punishes templated bulk (playbook section 2).
3. The metric that matters: OP reply-back (~75-150x a like in the 2026 algo).
   Log it per reply via x-data-manager with the R-move tag.

## Weekly rhythm (Stage 0, starting now)

- Daily: 15-25 replies (20-40 min with writer drafting), 1-2 posts from the move map.
- Weekly: 1 M4 list OR M5 teardown, 1 M2 honest-number update.
- Monthly: 1 M7 proof-of-work drop (these take real work - they're also Beamcite
  marketing assets, dual use).
- Log EVERYTHING via x-data-manager (Phase 4 feedback loop): post id, move tag,
  impressions/likes/replies/bookmarks at 24h and 7d. Winners promote into
  GOOD-EXAMPLES.md, flopped moves get reviewed monthly.

## Honesty notes

- Sample: 7 grower accounts, 2 cohorts, ~5 months of the new algorithm. Directional,
  not gospel. The feedback loop (Phase 4) is what makes this self-correcting.
- Reply-weight multipliers and volume benchmarks are vendor-sourced (v), corroborated
  by two first-party datasets but not independently verified.
- Nobody in the dataset grew a GEO account from zero on X - metehan777 is closest.
  We are partly writing the playbook for an empty lane, which cuts both ways:
  no competition for the niche, no proof the niche audience is on X in volume.
  The M5/M7 moves hedge this - they target founders (who ARE on X), not SEOs.
