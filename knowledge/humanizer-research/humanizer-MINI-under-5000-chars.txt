HUMANIZER. Rewrite AI text so it reads human. Never change a fact, never invent
a specific; if a detail is missing write [SPECIFIC: what]. Minimum passes. No
em-dashes. Report confidence per claim. Accuracy beats human-ness. Aim at the
expert bar: heavy LLM users catch AI ~93% on vocabulary and formulaic shape.

1 STRIP.
Words, delete where meaning survives else swap plain: delve, underscore,
intricate, showcase, boasts, crucial, pivotal, comprehensive, utilize, align,
meticulous, tapestry, palpable, vibrant, significantly, realm, groundbreaking,
garner, innovative, leverage, harness, foster, bolster, enhance, streamline,
facilitate, empower, unveil, unlock, navigate, encompass, seamless, robust,
transformative, invaluable, nuanced, holistic, multifaceted, paramount, myriad,
compelling, notably, effectively, ultimately, landscape, insights, testament.
Flag list, not ban list: swap only where the word does puffery work. One use is
fine; the tell is repetition.
Phrases, rewrite from the point, never word-swap: "in today's fast-paced world",
"when it comes to", "it's important to note that", "at the end of the day", "is
a testament to", "plays a crucial role in", fake "from X to Y" ranges, "In
conclusion", "a game-changer".
Transitions, vary not delete: Additionally/Moreover/Furthermore -> Also, And,
Plus or cut. However -> But, Still. Therefore/Thus -> So. Uniform connective
stacking is itself the tell.
Break nominalizations ("the utilization of X" -> "using X"). Cut trailing
participle tags ("..., highlighting the importance of Y").

2 RHYTHM. Force sentence-length variance, no two adjacent sentences the same
length. Variance reads human, not a short average.

3 INJECT. Stripped text is flat and still reads AI. Add hedges where the author
would truly be unsure (maybe, probably, I think, seems like, tends to) and
stance (honestly, to be fair, the catch is, annoyingly). Add first person and
one aside. Take ONE position, do not both-sides it. Let one part run warmer. Add
a concrete specific only if it is in the source.

4 VOICE. The biggest lever. ONE coherent persona with a stable idiolect, never a
scatter of quirks; experts catch performed humanity. With samples, copy sentence
length and variance, openers, contraction rate, hedge rate, punctuation and
formality. Copy habits, never content. Keep imperfection consistent, never
sprinkled. A pass that skips this stage is INCOMPLETE and gets no score.

5 TEST. Read as a hostile expert: quote every machine signal left, say what a
human would do, score 0-100, separately list any claim that looks invented. Loop
to step 1 until clean. A draft's own "all clear" note is not a test.

GREP THESE, they survive the steps above.
BANNED: negation contrast in any form including headings ("It's not X. It's Y",
"X, not Y"). Grep ", not ".
BANNED: antithesis that survives that grep, two balanced positive lines sorting
the world into halves ("One tells you A / The other tells you B"). Check
adjacent paragraphs for matching -ing subjects.
Mic-drop closer on every section; cap at 2, end some flat.
Labeled pivots and colons in body prose ("The honest caveat:").
A FAQ or summary restating a body fact with only the adjectives changed.
Uniform 25-45 word sentences in the intro or TL;DR.
Perfect 3-part or 4-part parallel builds, and polished tricolons.
Comma-spliced appositive stacks, the noun-then-three-examples dump.
Symmetric anaphora, adjacent or at paragraph scale. Same-verb runs.
More than one fragment run; under 500 words, over two and terseness is the tell.
Echo-stamp verdicts flipping the previous noun back ("Motivation does.").
Symmetric equations ("every minute of X is also a minute of Y").
One figurative phrase used 3 times in a piece.
Sibling lines sharing one clause shape and one length band with no concrete
noun. List their first words and character counts; clustering means generated.
Stock format openers ("In this article"). Ask what THIS material makes a
specific person say first.

OVER 400 WORDS. Never one pass over the whole thing; the model drifts back to
the AI register mid-document. Segment on real structure, never touching
headings, code, quotes, tables, numbers, names, dates. Lock ONE voice spec
before section one and reuse it verbatim. Run 1-3 per section, 4 with that spec,
varying which human move each section uses. Then a document pass: section
averages must differ, grep every stripped word across the piece (5 or more hits
means vary it), cap repeated openers, break identical section shapes, let one
section run warmer. Then the critic over the WHOLE document, then a fact pass.

NEVER USE burstiness score thresholds, banned-word lists beyond the above,
"uniform paragraph lengths" as a tell, or typos as a trick.

OUTPUT. The text with placeholders, what changed by stage, the 0-100 score, a
fact-check note, confidence per claim.

CAVEAT. Delve, intricate and underscore have been fading since April 2024, so
phrase tells and flat register now beat any single word.
