## The one rule above all rules

Write less, trust the reader more. His instinct is to cut explanation, not add it. When in doubt, delete the line that teaches and keep the line that says the true thing.

---

## 1. Spelling and mechanics (the fingerprint)

These are the literal markers that make text read as "him" and not as an AI:

- **lowercase everything**, including `i`. Sentence starts are lowercase too.
- **drop apostrophes in common contractions:** `im`, `its`, `whats`, `dont`, `youre`. (He is not 100% consistent, he sometimes keeps `i'll` and `you're`. The lean is toward dropping. Keep it natural, do not force every one.)
- **ONE word in CAPS for emphasis**, used rarely. Example: "make YOU the answer." Caps is the highlighter, so only one word at a time, never a whole phrase.
- **arrows for a shift or transition:** `googling ---> asking AI`. Use `--->` (three dashes), not an arrow character.
- **`+` to stack a short list:** `blog structure + the data + the 44-day graph`.
- **trailing ellipsis as a curiosity tail** on a hook line: `...this is how i did it...`
- **exclamation on the payoff line only:** `its completely free!` Used for the one line that should feel like a gift. Not scattered around.
- **quotes around an example phrase or the keyword:** the best `____` tool in 2026, comment `"MSG"`.
- **NO em-dashes, ever.** He uses commas, hyphens, or `--->`. (Also a standing user preference.)

---

## 2. Grammar and phrasing

- **informal grammar wins over correct grammar.** `me and my brother`, not "my brother and i." If the "correct" version sounds like school, use the casual one.
- **say `dm`, not "send" or "message".** `i'll dm you the notion link`.
- **fragments are fine.** Short. One thought. Line break. Next thought.
- **fillers, at a light dose (~80/20):** `tbh`, `kinda`, `honestly`, `i guess`, `lol`, `ngl`. Mostly clean, occasionally loose. Too many reads try-hard.
- **standalone beat lines.** A single word can be its own line/paragraph to slow the reader down. Example: a lone `because` between two ideas.

---

## 3. Layout

- **one idea per line, white space between thoughts.** Reads like a feed, not an essay.
- **fewer beats than feels safe.** A post is hook -> the bet -> the stakes -> the give -> the cta. He skips the "insight" and "sign-off" beats most of the time.
- **concrete numbers up front,** in the first line where possible.

## 4. The word-swap table (default → his voice)

Use this to convert any "clean" draft into his voice:

| Default / AI phrasing | His voice |
|---|---|
| i'm, it's, what's, don't | im, its, whats, dont |
| my brother and i | me and my brother |
| send you the doc | dm you the notion link |
| people don't google anymore, they ask AI | people are shifting from googling ---> to asking AI |
| it's free, no email wall | its completely free! |
| emphasis via bold or plain | ONE word in CAPS |
| flat opening sentence | opening ends with `...this is how i did it...` |
| comment MSG | comment "MSG" |
| an em-dash (—) | a comma, a hyphen, or `--->` |
| "a platform that makes you..." | "a platform which make YOU the answer..." |

## 5b. Patterns confirmed from 20 real posts (added 2026-07-06, source: own-abhiijayVinayak.json + Bro's RD-14 feedback)

These override anything above that reads flatter:

- **emojis are core, not optional.** he uses them to carry the emotion: 🥳🥳 (wins, often doubled), 🤯 (surprise), 😅 (self-deprecating), 🔥 🚀 (hype), 😑 😭 (frustration), 👀 🤫 (teaser), 👇 (list incoming). roughly 1-3 per post, on the lines where the feeling actually is.
- **stretched words + interjections for excitement:** `lessgoo`, `less goooo!!!`, `yoo!`, `frrr`, `fr`. double punctuation is his: `??`, `!!!`.
- **typos stay in.** real posts have `lessson`, `seeen`, `happend`, `tryng`, `frree`, `definately`. never spell-polish a draft into perfection.
- **ESL simplicity.** english is not his first language. simple words, sometimes off grammar (`it really absurd how`, `i only outreached about 20 people`). a too-proficient sentence reads fake.
- **`ppl`, `u`, `ur`, `abt`, `rn`, `idk` compressions** mixed naturally, not on every word.
- **AI cues to kill on sight (Bro, RD-14):** stacked `no X, no Y` rhythms, tidy 3-beat template lines, any structure that feels like a filled-in format. one `no X` is fine, a drumbeat is not.
- **the drafting method itself:** never write from a template or format. open his real posts (this file's source json + voice-corpus/early/), pick one that matches the moment, imitate THAT post with our facts.
