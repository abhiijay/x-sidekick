# k77builds Voice Rules

Voice spec for the `@k77builds` X (Twitter) personal founder account.
Read this before drafting any tweet for `persona: k77builds`.
This file is mutable. Edit freely as the voice evolves.

> **READ FIRST for hook/shareability:** `guides/viral-content-psychology-guide.md`.
> For X specifically: Principle 2 (curiosity gap - the first line IS the hook) and
> Principle 5 (design for the share, not the like). Voice rules below override the
> guide's tone suggestions where they conflict.

## Account Context

- **Handle:** @k77builds
- **Display name:** k77 · building supasidebar
- **Origin story:** k77 = university roll number 77, kept it
- **Positioning:** Personal founder account. NOT a brand account.
- **Why separate from main:** Bro doesn't want his existing friend network to see early-stage build-in-public. Authenticity is the entire point of this account.
- **Public goal:** 50k SupaSidebar downloads by August 2026
- **Public climb narrative:** 2024 (-$15k, left uni, no job) → 2025 (+$22k, sold first product) → 2026 (chasing first $100k via SSB)

## Tone

- **Honest climb.** Real numbers when shareable, real failures when relevant.
- **Direct.** No fake humility. No fake hustle. No LinkedIn energy.
- **First-person solo founder.** "I" not "we." "I'm building" not "we're shipping."
- **Sometimes blunt.** It's OK to say things plainly even if they sound rough.
- **Never performative.** If a post feels like a performance, don't post it.

## Hook Style

Default to one of these five hook types. Tag the hook type in frontmatter for analytics.

| Hook Type | When to use | Example shape |
|-----------|------------|---------------|
| `number` | Revenue, downloads, time-bound milestones | "spent 6 months on a feature 12 people use" |
| `confession` | Vulnerability, what's not working, fear | "I almost took down my account today because" |
| `claim` | Strong opinion or counter-narrative | "indie founders are wasting time on landing pages" |
| `story` | Specific moment, anchored in time | "got the email this morning saying my first sub renewed" |
| `question` | Genuine curiosity, not rhetorical | "what's the smallest thing you shipped that mattered most?" |

**Avoid as default:** "Thread 🧵" hooks, "let me tell you a story" preambles, "I just realized" openers.

## Length

- **Default:** Single tweet. If you can't say it in 280 chars, the idea isn't sharp enough.
- **Threads:** Only when the idea genuinely needs more space (multi-step build log, post-mortem, deep teardown). Aim for 4-7 tweets max.
- **Image/screenshot:** When showing a product moment, screen, or chart, lead with the image.

## SSB Mention Rule

- **Target:** 50-70% of posts mention SupaSidebar in some form (link, screenshot, name-drop, feature demo).
- **30-50% are pure build-in-public:** revenue updates, climb-narrative posts, founder reflections, learnings - no product mention.
- **Never mention SSB just to mention it.** Forced mentions read as ads.
- **Always link in pinned bio, not in every tweet.** Tweets with links get downranked by the algorithm.

## Forbidden Phrases

Mutable. Edit as you notice things slipping in.

- Em-dashes (use hyphens or commas - this is a global Bro preference)
- "🧵" (thread emoji)
- "Game-changer"
- "I'm thrilled to share"
- "Excited to announce"
- "Massive shoutout"
- "Huge for us"
- "Stay tuned"
- "Drop a comment if"
- "Like and retweet"
- "1/" as a thread starter (use line break + content directly)
- Generic founder platitudes ("the journey", "the grind", "trust the process")

## Required When Relevant

- **Real numbers.** Don't sandbag, don't inflate. If you don't want to share the exact number, don't share a number at all.
- **Specific over vague always.** "$22 MRR" beats "growing slowly." "12 people downloaded yesterday" beats "got some traction."
- **Time anchors.** "today", "this morning", "last week" - locate the moment.
- **First-person attribution.** "I built", "I broke", "I learned" - own it.

## What This Account Is NOT For

- Product marketing copy (that's `@supasidebar` brand account)
- Replying to support questions (use `@supasidebar`)
- Press release-style announcements
- Tweeting at famous people for engagement bait
- Subtweeting competitors
- Hot takes outside the indie/build space
