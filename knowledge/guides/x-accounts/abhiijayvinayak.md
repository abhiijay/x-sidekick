## Venue before shape (HARD, added 2026-07-31)

**Decide WHERE the post will live before picking the move.** Venue is chosen
first, shape second. A shape that works in one venue is dead in the other, and
the move-picker in the x-post-writer guide cannot see the difference.

| Venue | What works | What dies |
|---|---|---|
| Build in Public community | Day-N / streak posts, journey updates, honest small numbers, moments. The room already knows the format, so no context is needed. Beamcite stays BACKGROUND, never pitched (community rules ban self-promotion, engagement farming, and generic reply bait). | Anything that reads as a pitch or a broadcast. |
| General feed | Posts that carry their own context to a stranger with zero prior. | Day-N posts (a stranger has no idea what day 34 means). Receipt / insight / analyst posts, unless a bigger audience is actively being routed in - they only pay off when someone is already looking. |

**Relatability beats insight in a peer room.** The audience is other small
builders and they engage with mirrors, not analysis. When the virality check
marks P3 (reader identity / self-recognition) WEAK, that is a blocking flag on
this account, not a note. Fix it or change the post.

**Questions must be easy-OPINION, never knowledge-shame.** A question is
engageable when answering it makes the replier look good ("what channel would u
bet on", RD-15 family). A question whose honest answer is "never" or "i don't
know what that is" gets zero replies and quietly insults the reader. Evidence:
"when did u last open ur robots.txt" failed twice - most founders do not know
what robots.txt is, so there is no recognition, and admitting it costs them.

**When an angle dies twice, the angle is dead.** Re-wrapping the same material a
third and fourth time is itself a failure mode, not persistence. Go find new
material instead. Evidence: the 2026-07-30 Canva robots.txt angle was punched up
through four iterations and the last one read more AI than the first.

**Feed-mix variety is a cap, not a guideline.** STAGE-PLAYBOOK Stage 0 mix:
streak post most days, ONE value post per week, MAXIMUM one news/stat teaching
post per week. Jul 27 workspace video + Jul 29 32-tools video + Jul 30 crawler
post broke the cap twice in four days. Count the last 7 days before drafting a
value or teaching post.
