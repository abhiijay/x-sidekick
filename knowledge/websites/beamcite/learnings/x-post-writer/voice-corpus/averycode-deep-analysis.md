# How Avery Actually Writes - Deep Analysis (@averycode)

Companion to `averycode.md` (summary + top posts). This is the full mechanics teardown plus the SKELETON LIBRARY. It is written to be self-sufficient: a fresh session with zero context should be able to write in this voice from this file alone. The post-shape analysis remains based on the original 404-post set captured through Jul 9 2026. The reply register was refreshed to 1,070 replies through Aug 9 2026 (`deep-averycode.jsonl`). Every number is measured, not vibes.

**How to use this file:** read Part A once to load the voice. When drafting, go to Part B, pick the skeleton that matches the day's material, and fill it with OUR facts. Then check against Part C (the fingerprint checklist). Never invent facts to fit a skeleton; pick a different skeleton instead.

# PART A - THE VOICE

## A1. The one-sentence take

She writes like someone texting a friend a good update: one thought per line, no periods, outcome first, and she stops before explaining. The polish is in the RESTRAINT, not in fancy words.

## A2. The architecture of a post

- A line is SHORT: median 38 characters, 90% under 93. That is 5-10 words.
- A post is 1 to 7 lines. Most common: 1 line (29%), then 3-4 lines (41%). Never more than ~8.
- One thought = one line, blank line between thoughts. If a sentence needs a comma to keep going, she breaks the line instead.
- She front-loads. Line 1 of the quit-my-job post is "I finally quit my 9-5 job", not "After 10 years of thinking about it...". The 10 years is line 2.
- The last line hands the ending to the reader: a link, a question, or a forward-looking line. Never a moral, never a lesson stamp.

## A3. The punctuation fingerprint (her biggest tell)

- **94% of lines end with NO punctuation.** Of 1,265 lines: 1,194 end bare, 46 with ?, 23 with !, ~0 with a period. The line break IS her period.
- ! about once every 17 posts, only on true peaks. ? only for genuine invites (41 of 404 posts end on a question).
- The mid-line hyphen is her turn signal: "It's more than I could've imagined - start building in public, your story matters" / "one day it'll be worth it". One turn per post, max.
- Never an em-dash in the whole corpus. Sentence case always, clean apostrophes, capital first letter. Only 1% of posts start lowercase.
- Almost no typos, but not zero: "3 month ago", "producteave" (a deliberate pun). She does not sand off every edge.

## A4. List markers - she has exactly three, each with a job

- **✅ / ☑️ = things DONE** (180 uses, she alternates the two glyphs). Only ever 1-3 items. Verb-first, no adjectives: "✅ Fixed gaps in tracking".
- **> = data or steps** (92 uses). Used for before/after stat stacks and how-to steps: "> 30 followers / > 0 impressions".
- **- = features of a thing she built** (the tool posts): "- Paste any video link (or multiple)".
- **+ inside a line = stacking ingredients** (122 uses): "rork + chatgpt", "opus 4.5 + rork + pinterest".
Never numbered lists. Never more than ~5 list items except the rare full-inventory post.

## A5. The emoji system

- 39% of posts have ZERO emoji, 34% exactly one, 21% two. More than three is rare. Placement beats quantity: the emoji sits on the feeling line, never on a fact line.
- Palette, ranked by actual use: ✅/☑️ (structural), 🥹 (36, touched/grateful - HER signature), 🎉 (28, milestone), 🤯 (17, the artifact wow), 💨 (15, momentum), 😮‍💨 (13, tired-but-fine), 🔥 (13), 🫡 (10, soldiering on), plus skin-toned 🙌🏼 👇🏼 🫶🏻 🤞🏼 🙏🏼 and one-offs like 😳 (shock at a number), 👀 (teaser), ☺️ (soft close).
- No 😂💀 comedy layer at all. When something is funny she says it plainly ("or should i say producteave") and lets it be funny.

## A6. Parentheses - three jobs, 32 uses total

1. **Credibility whisper:** the how, compressed: "(three.js + mediapipe)", "(built with opus 4.5 on rork)", "(8x8 grid, block shapes and sizes)".
2. **Objection pre-empt:** kill the top reply-guy comment before it lands: "($0 rent (living with parents)", "(~1200 USD)", "(trial + paid)", "(<1 year)".
3. **Playful aside:** "(wrong answers only)", "(please)", "(hopefully)", "(first run post injury, a disaster 😭 but progress nonetheless)".
If a parenthesis is explaining rather than whispering, pre-empting, or winking, it should not exist.

## A7. Numbers style

Exact and personal, never rounded to vagueness: "$100 MRR", "160+ installs", "11% download to trial conversion rate", "950+ waitlist users", "17k followers", "50m+ impressions". The + suffix does the hedging ("400+", "100k+"). ~ only for others' numbers ("~$1M/day"). One comic exaggeration ever ("+1,000,000,000% 😳") - allowed exactly because everything else is sober. After a good number, she often adds the honest downside line: "Most users cancel the trial right after starting - will be iterating on onboarding to reduce this". That line is why the numbers are believed.

## A8. Vocabulary - what she says and never says

- First person: "I" 198, "my" 162. Reader-facing: "you/your" 133. "we" 13 (solo voice).
- Verbs: building (199), built (22), launched (12). She NEVER says "shipped" as the verb of record (0 uses in her own posts; one "shipped 6 apps" inside a stat stack). No indie-hacker jargon dialect.
- Near-zero intensifiers: "literally" 4 (always doing real work), "honestly" 2, "actually" rare. Delete these from drafts by default.
- Feeling words are plain: fun (19), love (12), excited (9), feel (27). Emotional peaks: "unreal", "can't believe", "grateful", "thankful", "it feels unreal", "one day it'll be worth it", "changed my life". Mild slang, rare and current: "goated", "locked in".
- Gratitude is a real content category: "Thank you" opens 14 posts, often standalone: "Thank you so much for 6000 🥹".

## A9. Self-templating - her hidden production trick

She repeats HER OWN winning posts nearly verbatim, weeks apart, with updated numbers. The before/after post ("100 days ago > 30 followers... Now > 9k... this is your sign ✨") ran at day ~100, day 142, and month 9, each time a hit. The streak scoreboard is the same skeleton daily for months. The lesson for us: once a post of ours works, RERUN the skeleton with fresh numbers every 4-6 weeks. She does not invent a new format when a proven one has room left.

## A10. How she frames a story (the selection method)

1. Find the one moment with feeling in it, not the task list: the birthday spent debugging, the nephew asking "can you teach me how to make apps", 14 weekends in a row indoors.
2. State the outcome in line 1, cold. No windup.
3. One beat of context. Ten years compressed to one line; the mechanism compressed to a parenthesis.
4. Hand the ending to the reader: link, question, or forward line ("Full speed ahead 💨", "Ready for a year of adventure"). The reader supplies the meaning; that is why they reply.
5. Pre-empt the one obvious objection yourself when numbers are involved.
6. Negative space: no threads, no hashtags, no "agree?" bait, no lesson listicles (except after a WIN, see S11), no selling inside the streak. The product appears only as "what I did today".

## A11. The two registers (never mixed)

| | POSTS (original analyzed set: 404) | REPLIES (current merged set: 1,070) |
|---|---|---|
| Case | 99% sentence case | 95.79% lowercase after mentions |
| Length | median 159 chars | median body 33 chars, 6 words |
| Lines | shaped, usually multi-line | 90.19% one line |
| Energy | composed, shaped | warm, fast, spontaneous |
| Emoji | 0-2, placed | present in 43.55%: 🙌🏼 🫡 🥹 😭 ☺️ |
| Questions | invitation when the post earns it | 64 of 1,070, or 5.98% |
| `wait` opener | not a post marker | 2 of 1,070, or 0.19% |
| Moves | the skeletons below | acknowledgment, reaction, thanks, congrats, micro-confession, rare genuine question |

Reply examples, verbatim: "thanks for the mention aditi 🫡 following!" / "congrats robin! first 100 was honestly the hardest" / "ooo that’s so good!" / "yeaa tough to recover from :(" / "how bout you??" / "oh i definitely didn't skip the reply guy arc 😭 i used to reply so much x would temporarily ban me as bot activity". Formula: recognize the person or exact moment, add one real beat, get out. The feed looks curated, the comment section proves a human. Deliberate trust machine.

Anti-template constraint: a memorable example is evidence that a move exists, not a reusable opening. `wait what are the cameras for??` is one of only two `wait`-opening replies in the 1,070-reply corpus. Never convert it into `wait + question` as the Avery default. Questions of any kind appear in only 5.98% of the corpus. Most Avery replies should be a short reaction, acknowledgment, thanks, congratulations, agreement, or micro-confession. For Abhiijay output, actual `posted_text` outranks copied Avery mannerisms.

## A12. Cadence and the weekly mix

9.1 posts per week over 312 days, no gaps. Rough weekly mix: 3-4 streak posts (S1-S3), 1-2 progress or milestone posts (S4, S9), ~1 life or gratitude post (S13, S14), a question when she has a real one (S8), and an artifact/tool/before-after post only when she actually has one. Scarcity is the point: 7 artifact posts in 10 months is WHY they average 786 likes.

Engagement per shape (median likes): artifact 786 (n=7), breakdown 232 (n=9), day streak 133 (n=140), bare question 121 (n=27), everything else 84 (n=221).

# PART C - THE FINGERPRINT CHECKLIST (run on every draft)

1. Line 1 = the outcome or moment, under ~10 words, no setup
2. One thought per line, median ~38 chars, blank line between thoughts, max 7 lines
3. NO periods at line ends. ! only on a real peak (max one). ? only for a real invite
4. Sentence case, clean apostrophes, no em-dashes anywhere
5. List markers by job: ✅ done / > data & steps / - features / + stacking. 1-3 ✅ max
6. 0-2 emoji, on the feeling line only. Palette: ✅ 🥹 🎉 🤯 💨 😮‍💨 🫡 🙌🏼. No 😂💀
7. Numbers exact with + hedge (160+, 100k+); after a win, add the honest downside line
8. Parens only whisper (stack), pre-empt (objection), or wink (aside)
9. Kill on sight: "literally/honestly/actually" as filler, "shipped" as the verb, threads, hashtags, morals, "curious to hear your thoughts", any CTA inside a streak post
10. Close on link / question / forward line. The reader finishes the post, not you
11. Skeleton matches the material. No artifact = no S6. No fresh win = no S11. Nothing fits = don't post that idea
12. Replies switch register: lowercase, ~35 chars, name the person, one beat
13. Abhijay constants on top of her voice: Beamcite facts verified same-run, her life facts never transfer, punch up never down. "I'm 17" is NOT a per-post constant (RD-16, 2026-07-10): Avery never puts her credential in post bodies, the bio does that job. Age line only when age is the point, ~1 in 5 max
14. Rerun proven skeletons with fresh numbers every 4-6 weeks (S5 especially) - self-repeat is method, not laziness
