## Her reply register (1,070 replies - the second voice)

Her replies are a DIFFERENT register from her posts, and the split is disciplined:

- 95.79% start lowercase after leading mentions (posts are sentence-case). The polish is for the feed; replies are typed fast and warm.
- Median reply body is 33 chars and 6 words. 90.19% are one line. One thought, done.
- Names the person: "thanks for the mention aditi 🫡" / "all the best rede! 🙌🏼"
- Emoji carries the feeling when there is one: 🙌🏼 🫡 🥹 😭 ☺️. Emoji appears in 43.55% of reply bodies, not every reply.
- Honest micro-confessions in replies: "i used to reply so much x would temporarily ban me as bot activity 😭"
- Questions are rare: 64 of 1,070 replies, or 5.98%.
- `wait` is not an Avery opener. Only 2 of 1,070 replies begin with it, or 0.19%.
- The two real `wait` examples are exceptions: "wait what are the cameras for??" and "wait what does this mean!!"
- Common real starts are `thanks`, `i`, `it`, `appreciate`, `let`, `love`, `that`, `this`, `thank`, `you`, `yes`, `haha`, `yep`, `congrats`, and `same`.
- Recent examples show the range: "ooo that’s so good!" / "yeaa tough to recover from :(" / "congrats on the launch!" / "how bout you??" / "thanks lynn :)".

Takeaway: post register = polished sentence-case shapes; reply register = lowercase warm one-beat reactions. Two tools, never mixed.

### Anti-template rule for Avery replies

- Do not start an Avery draft with `wait` unless the target produced genuine immediate confusion or surprise. Even then, prefer the clean question without `wait` when it still sounds natural.
- Do not force a question to manufacture conversation. A question needs a real missing detail that the writer actually wants answered.
- Default to the one honest beat the post naturally earns: acknowledgment, reaction, congratulations, agreement, micro-confession, or a specific question.
- Do not reuse the same opener across the three options or across adjacent targets.
- A corpus example proves that a move is possible. It is never a fill-in template.
